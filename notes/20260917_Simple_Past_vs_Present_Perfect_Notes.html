Simple Past vs. Present Perfect

一般过去时 vs. 现在完成时｜Ricky ESL 3–4 专题笔记 · 时间线 · 对比例句 · 美式发音 · Quick Check

🎯 先抓住最核心的区别

Simple Past｜一般过去时

过去发生，而且放在一个已经结束的过去时间里。
V2 / V-ed
PAST　● DONE ─────────────→ NOW
记忆：What happened in the past? 过去发生了什么？

Present Perfect｜现在完成时

事情与过去有关，但站在现在 NOW看它：结果、经历，或者持续到现在。
have / has + V3
PAST ─── ✔ / experience ───→ NOW
记忆：What is true up to now? 到现在为止怎么样了？

🟠 Part 1｜Simple Past 一般过去时

基本结构

肯定句
Subject + V2 / V-ed
I studied English yesterday.
我昨天学了英语。
否定句
Subject + did not (didn't) + V1
I didn't study English yesterday.
我昨天没有学英语。
疑问句
Did + subject + V1 ...?
Did you study yesterday?
你昨天学习了吗？
⚠ did 已经表示过去，后面的动词回到 V1：
❌ Did you went?　✅ Did you go?

什么时候使用？

动作发生在明确而且已经结束的过去时间。
常见时间词
yesterday · last night · last week · ago · in 2024 · when I was...
We went hiking last Saturday.
我们上周六去徒步了。
She called me yesterday.
她昨天给我打电话了。
I visited Japan in 2024.
我2024年去了日本。
I studied English for two years.
我以前学了两年英语。（现在已经不学了）

🔵 Part 2｜Present Perfect 现在完成时

基本结构

肯定
Subject + have / has + V3
I have finished my homework.
我已经完成作业了。
否定
Subject + have / has not + V3
I haven't finished yet.
我还没有完成。
疑问
Have / Has + subject + V3 ...?
Have you ever tried Korean food?
你吃过韩国菜吗？
❌ I have went.　✅ I have gone.
Present Perfect 要用 V3，不是 V2。

三个最重要的用法

A｜过去发生 → 现在有结果
I have lost my keys.
我的钥匙丢了。
↳ 现在仍然找不到钥匙。
B｜截止现在的人生经历
I have been to Japan three times.
我去过日本三次。
C｜过去开始 → 持续到现在
I have known her for ten years.
我认识她十年了。
常见提示词
already · just · yet · ever · never · before · so far · for · since

⚖️ Part 3｜直接对比

① lost vs. have lost

I lost my keys yesterday.
我昨天弄丢了钥匙。→ 重点是昨天发生的事。
I have lost my keys.
我的钥匙丢了。→ 重点是现在没有钥匙。

② went vs. have been

I went to Japan in 2024.
明确过去时间 → Simple Past
I have been to Japan three times.
截止现在的人生经历 → Present Perfect

③ lived vs. have lived

I lived there for three years.
通常表示现在已经不住那里。
I have lived here for three years.
三年前开始，现在仍住这里。

⏰ Part 4｜时间词为什么重要？

已经结束的过去时间

yesterday / last year / ago / in 2024 → 通常用 Simple Past。
❌ I have been to Japan last year.
✅ I went to Japan last year.

还没结束的时间段

today / this week / this year 有时可用 Present Perfect，表示“到现在为止”。
I have had two cups of coffee today.
我今天到现在已经喝了两杯咖啡。
⚠ 不是看到 today / this week 就自动用完成时。仍要看说话人的时间视角。

📍 Part 5｜for 和 since

for = 多久

for + 一段时间

I have lived here for three years.

since = 从什么时候开始

since + 起点

I have lived here since 2023.

对比：I lived there for three years. → 那段居住已经结束。
I have lived here for three years. → 通常表示现在还住这里。

🧭 Part 7｜怎么判断？

STEP 1
事情与过去有关吗？
STEP 2
有明确、已经结束的过去时间？
YES → Simple Past
STEP 3
重点是现在结果、经历或持续到现在？
YES → Present Perfect
Ricky 记忆法： Simple Past → “什么时候发生的？”　Present Perfect → “到现在怎么样了？”

📝 Part 8｜Quick Check｜点击 Show Answer

1. I _____ (go) to San Diego last weekend.
Show Answerwent — last weekend 已经结束 → Simple Past。
2. I _____ never _____ (try) Indian food.
Show Answerhave never tried — 截止现在的人生经历。
3. She _____ (finish) her homework an hour ago.
Show Answerfinished — an hour ago 明确指过去。
4. _____ you ever _____ (see) snow?
Show AnswerHave you ever seen — ever 询问人生经历。
5. We _____ (live) here since 2023.
Show Answerhave lived — 从过去持续到现在。
6. My friend _____ (call) me yesterday.
Show Answercalled — yesterday → Simple Past。
7. I can't find my keys. I _____ (lose) them.
Show Answerhave lost — 过去丢失造成现在“找不到”的结果。
8. I _____ (study) English for two years when I was in China, but then I stopped.
Show Answerstudied — then I stopped 表明学习已经结束。
9. I _____ (know) her for ten years, and we are still good friends.
Show Answerhave known — 状态持续到现在。
10. A. I have visited New York in 2024.　B. I visited New York in 2024.
Show AnswerB — in 2024 是明确、已经结束的过去时间。
