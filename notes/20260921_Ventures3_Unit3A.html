ladder

n. 名词/ˈlædər/
中文释义：梯子

Quizlet 英文释义：A frame with steps used for climbing up or down high places. A noun.

高频搭配与例句
  • climb a ladder（爬梯子）
  • go up / down a ladder（爬上 / 爬下梯子）
  • a tall ladder（高梯子）
记忆提示

看到 ladder，就想象一把有很多横档的梯子：climb a ladder。

😠

complain

v. 动词/kəmˈpleɪn/
中文释义：抱怨；投诉

Quizlet 英文释义：To say that you are unhappy with something. To show dissatisfaction. A verb.

高频搭配与例句
  • complain about something（抱怨某事）
  • complain to someone（向某人投诉）
  • stop complaining（别再抱怨）
记忆提示

重点记：complain about + 事情；complain to + 人。

🤝

favor

n. 名词/ˈfeɪvər/
中文释义：帮助；恩惠；好事

Quizlet 英文释义：A kind or helpful thing that you do for someone. A good deed. A noun.

高频搭配与例句
  • do someone a favor（帮某人一个忙）
  • ask for a favor（请别人帮忙）
  • return the favor（回报这个人情）
记忆提示

最实用的句子：Can you do me a favor? 你能帮我一个忙吗？

💵

owe

v. 动词/oʊ/
中文释义：欠（钱）；欠（人情）

Quizlet 英文释义：To need to pay money back to someone, or to need to do something in return for a favor. To be in debt. A verb.

高频搭配与例句
  • owe someone money（欠某人钱）
  • owe $20（欠20美元）
  • owe someone a favor（欠某人一个人情）
记忆提示

owe 强调“欠着还没有还”：I owe you $10. 我欠你10美元。

Quizlet 英文释义：A sound, especially one that is loud, unpleasant, or unwanted. A racket or terrible sound. A noun.

高频搭配与例句

noise 是名词“噪音”。看到 🔊，想到：Don’t make too much noise.

lend

v. 动词/lend/
中文释义：借给；借出

Quizlet 英文释义：To give something to someone for a short time, then they give it back to you. Loan out, give temporarily or allow the use of. A verb.

高频搭配与例句
  • lend someone something（把某物借给某人）
  • lend something to someone（把某物借给某人）
  • Can you lend me $10?（你能借给我10美元吗？）
记忆提示

lend = 借出去。东西从“我”这里出去：I lend it to you.

because

conj. 连词/bɪˈkɔːz/
中文释义：因为

Quizlet 英文释义：Used to give a reason or explain why something happens. Since, as, due to the fact. A conjunction.

高频搭配与例句
  • because + sentence（because 后接句子）
  • because I was tired（因为我累了）
  • because it was raining（因为当时在下雨）
记忆提示

看到 Why...? 就想到 because 来回答原因：Why? — Because...

中文释义：借入；借用

Quizlet 英文释义：To take and use something that is someone else's, promising to return it later. To use temporarily. A verb.

高频搭配与例句
  • borrow something from someone（从某人那里借某物）
  • borrow a book（借一本书）

appreciate

v. 动词/əˈpriːʃieɪt/
中文释义：感激；欣赏；珍惜

Quizlet 英文释义：To feel thankful or grateful for something or someone. To value or treasure. A verb.

高频搭配与例句
  • I really appreciate it.（我真的很感激。）
  • appreciate your help（感谢你的帮助）
  • appreciate what you have（珍惜你所拥有的）
记忆提示

实用表达：I really appreciate your help. 我真的很感谢你的帮助。

词汇记忆小故事

有一天，我的邻居（neighbor）来找我，因为他需要一把梯子（ladder）。他问我能不能把梯子借给（lend）他。

我说：“当然可以。”他很感激（appreciate）我的帮助。后来，我需要用他的工具，所以我去向他借（borrow）。

晚上，外面有很大的噪音（noise），我忍不住抱怨（complain）。邻居过来帮忙，我感谢他的这个帮助（favor）。

我开玩笑说：“现在我欠（owe）你一个人情，因为（because）你也帮助了我！”

A Vocabulary Memory Story

One day, my neighbor came to my house because he needed a ladder. He asked me to lend it to him.

I said, “Of course.” He really appreciated my help. Later, I needed one of his tools, so I asked to borrow it.

That evening, there was a lot of noise outside, and I started to complain. My neighbor came over to help. I thanked him for the favor.

I joked, “Now I owe you a favor because you helped me too!”
