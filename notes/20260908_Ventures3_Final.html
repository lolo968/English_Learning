reliable

adj. 形容词/rɪˈlaɪ.ə.bəl/
中文释义：可靠的、靠谱的

英文释义：Someone or something you can trust or depend on. Dependable, trustworthy.

高频搭配与例句
  • a reliable source of information (可靠的消息来源)
  • reliable method (可靠的方法)
  • be reliable at work (工作靠谱)
记忆提示

把 reliable 和 person / source / friend 绑定。想象身边最“靠谱”的朋友：He is a reliable friend.

🗣️

describe

v. 动词/dɪˈskraɪb/
中文释义：描述、形容

英文释义：To tell what something or someone is like. Explain.

高频搭配与例句
  • describe in detail (详细描述)
  • difficult to describe (难以描述)
  • describe A as B (把 A 描述 / 形容为 B)
记忆提示

口语和写作高频句：Words can’t describe how happy I am. 语言无法形容我现在有多高兴。

organized

adj. 形容词/ˈɔːr.ɡə.naɪzd/
中文释义：有条理的、组织严密的

英文释义：Arranged in order. Neat and well planned. Orderly or structured.

高频搭配与例句
  • well-organized plan (井井有条的计划)
  • highly organized person (非常有条理的人)
  • stay organized (保持有条不紊)
记忆提示

记住短语：Keep your desk organized. 保持你的桌子有条理。

conclusion

n. 名词/kənˈkluː.ʒən/
中文释义：结论、结尾

英文释义：The ending of something. Finish or result.

高频搭配与例句
  • in conclusion (总之 / 最后，写作常用)
  • jump to a conclusion (轻率下结论)
  • come to / reach a conclusion (得出结论)
记忆提示

直接把 in conclusion 作为一个整体短语记住，每次写文章总结时用一次就能记住。

中文释义：情况、局势、处境

英文释义：Something that is happening. State, condition or event.

高频搭配与例句
  • win-win situation (双赢)
  • current situation (现状 / 当前局势)
  • in a difficult situation (处于困境)
  • control the situation (掌控局势)
记忆提示

记住固定句型：It depends on the situation. 这取决于具体情况。

中文释义：专注、集中精力

英文释义：To give all your attention to what you are doing. To focus or pay attention.

高频搭配与例句
  • concentrate hard (高度集中注意力)
  • hard to concentrate (难以集中注意力)
  • concentrate on something / doing something (专心于某事 / 做某事)

pronounce

v. 动词/prəˈnaʊns/
中文释义：发音、读出

英文释义：To say a word correctly. To say, speak or articulate.

高频搭配与例句
  • hard to pronounce (难发音的)
  • pronounce correctly (正确发音)
  • How do you pronounce this word? (这个单词怎么发音？)
记忆提示

把 How do you pronounce this? 当成一句实用口语招牌句来背，遇到不会读的单词就能用上。

discourage

v. 动词/dɪsˈkɜːr.ɪdʒ/
中文释义：使沮丧、劝阻、打压

英文释义：To make someone lose confidence or want to stop trying. To deter or dissuade.

高频搭配与例句
  • feel discouraged (感到沮丧)
  • discourage someone from doing something (劝阻 / 阻止某人做某事)
  • Don’t let failures discourage you. (不要让失败打击到你)
记忆提示

记搭配 discourage … from …；不要把 discourage 和 discouraged 混淆：前者是“使某人沮丧”，后者常表示“感到沮丧”。

中文释义：词汇、词汇量

英文释义：The words that a person knows or learns. Terms or language.

高频搭配与例句
  • rich vocabulary (丰富的词汇量)
  • limited vocabulary (有限的词汇量)
  • expand / build your vocabulary (扩大你的词汇量)
  • increase my vocabulary (增加我的词汇量)
记忆提示

把动作和名词连起来背：build / expand / increase your vocabulary。

active

adj. 形容词/ˈæk.tɪv/
中文释义：积极的、活跃的

英文释义：Doing things, moving, or participating a lot. Involved, busy.

高频搭配与例句
  • active member (活跃成员)
  • active lifestyle (积极健康的生活方式)
  • take an active part in (积极参与……)
  • stay active (保持活力)
  • be active in class (课堂表现积极)
记忆提示

记日常短语 stay active（保持运动 / 保持活力）以及课堂高频句 be active in class（课堂表现积极）。

ESL课堂上的小故事

在 ESL 课堂上，有时我们会面对不同的情况（situation）。老师鼓励我们保持积极（active）。

老师教我们怎样正确发音（pronounce），也帮助我们增加词汇量（vocabulary）。

如果我们做事有条理（organized），并且上课时能够集中注意力（concentrate），学习就会更容易。遇到困难时，也不要感到沮丧（discouraged）。

找到适合自己的学习方法后，我们可以成为可靠的（reliable）学习者。我们也可以清楚地描述（describe）自己的想法，并在最后得出一个好的结论（conclusion）。

A Story in ESL Class

In ESL class, we sometimes face different situations. Our teacher tells us to stay active.

The teacher shows us how to pronounce words correctly. She also helps us build our vocabulary.

If we are organized and concentrate in class, learning becomes easier. When things are difficult, we should not feel discouraged.

With a good study method, we can become reliable learners. We can clearly describe our ideas and reach a good conclusion.
